> This is a sample entry for a collection of interests. It is not a completed review or a record of playing the game.

## Start with a fragment

Writing about a favorite work can begin with something small: a line of dialogue, a color palette, or an image that made you pause.

Instead of putting together a perfect list, it might be more interesting to start with why you want to keep it.

## Add a few words of your own

Different people notice different things in the same story. A collection page can provide a way into the work and a little space for your own response.
