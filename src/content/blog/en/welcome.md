## A space of your own

Some thoughts are best captured in a few words. Some stories deserve more time. A personal website can make room for both.

This one begins in simple black and white. There is space for the words, clear paths between pages, and a place for the things you like.

## Filling it in, slowly

You do not have to decide on everything on day one. Leave a sentence, add a project, then write a little about how things have been lately.

- **Writing**: Longer thoughts and shorter notes from daily life.
- **Projects**: Things you have tried and small projects still in progress.
- **Interests**: Games, stories, and other things you enjoy.

### Room for next time

A website can change along with your life. Today it is a simple index. Next time there might be another article, or a new color.

## A place to pause

The first step is having somewhere you can return to and keep writing.

Start here.
